# Career Future Stress Test: Software Developer (US)

## 1. Executive Summary
The occupation is the **most AI-exposed** in observed usage data — but the
observed pattern is transformation (augmentation-dominant) rather than
measured employment collapse. Risk concentrates in **entry-level, routine-task
segments**; resilience comes from moving up the task stack (architecture,
domain context, accountability). This is a `Model inference` built on S-level
exposure data; honest uncertainty is high.

## 5–9.
- Resilience: low-to-moderate at occupation level; moderate-to-high for the
  *individual* if positioned in high-dependency tasks (architecture, domain
  expertise, accountability).
- Main risk: entry-level ladder compression — the counter-consensus point:
  "AI-hot career" demand ↑ but graduate supply ↑↑ and entry tasks automated ↑.
- Main opportunity: complementarity — developers who operate AI well show
  productivity gains (augmentation share observed >50%).

## 10. Personal Fit

prioritize system design, domain depth, security, and AI-operation skills
(WEF 2025: AI/big-data skills fastest-growing, analytical thinking top core
skill; Observed survey, S).
